Numalog — free, private baby tracker app

Numalog is a free, private baby tracker app: log feeds, diapers, sleep and pumping one-handed. No account, no ads, works offline.

What you can track

  • Bottle and nursing feeds
  • Diapers
  • Sleep
  • Burp and tummy-time timers
  • Pumping
  • Growth on WHO charts
  • Vaccines, medicine and solids
  • Monthly photos and milestones
  • White noise and lullabies
  • Reminders
  • A summary PDF for your pediatrician
  • Backups

Questions

Is it free?

Yes, and there are no ads.

Do I need an account?

No. Open it and start logging — there is no sign-up.

Where is my data?

On your phone. Your entries stay on this device until you choose to share them. Family Sync, which shares one log between two phones, is opt-in.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. It runs in the browser on any phone, and you can add it to your home screen to open it like an app. The tracker needs JavaScript; the guides below do not.
